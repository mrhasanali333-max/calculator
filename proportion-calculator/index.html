About this calculator

Proportions express two equal ratios and are useful for scaling recipes, maps, rates, and quantities.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter A, B, and C.
  2. Leave D blank or enter the known values.
  3. Select Calculate to solve the proportion.
