About this calculator

A mortgage is a home loan repaid over time through principal and interest payments. Your down payment reduces the amount borrowed, while the rate and term affect the monthly payment and total interest.

Important note

Results are estimates and do not include every possible tax, insurance, fee, or lender condition.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the requested values using consistent currency and time units.
  2. Review the interest rate and loan term carefully.
  3. Select Calculate to see the estimated result.
  4. Use Reset to start another calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the results an offer of credit?

No. They are planning estimates. Confirm terms, fees, taxes, and insurance with a qualified lender.

Does a down payment change total interest?

Yes. A larger down payment generally reduces the principal and therefore the interest charged.
