About this calculator

VAT is a consumption tax applied at different stages of a supply chain. This tool shows the net amount, VAT amount, and gross total.

Important note

Use the VAT rate that applies to your country, product, or service.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter a price and VAT rate.
  2. Choose whether the price already includes VAT.
  3. Select Calculate and review the net and gross figures.

Formula and Calculation Method

Example Calculation

At 20% VAT, a net price of 100 has 20 VAT and a gross price of 120. If 120 is entered as VAT-inclusive, the tool reverses the same calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between net and gross?

Net is before VAT; gross is after VAT has been included.

Is VAT the same everywhere?

No. Rates, exemptions, and registration rules depend on jurisdiction.
