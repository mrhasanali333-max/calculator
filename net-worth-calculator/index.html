About this calculator

Net worth is a snapshot of what you own minus what you owe. Include current values rather than original purchase prices where practical.

Important note

Keep records private and use this as a personal planning estimate.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. List cash, accounts, investments, property, vehicles, and other assets.
  2. List credit cards, loans, and other debts.
  3. Select Calculate to compare assets and liabilities.

Formula and Calculation Method

Example Calculation

If total assets are 200,000 and total liabilities are 80,000, net worth is 120,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I include a mortgage?

Yes. Include property as an asset and the remaining mortgage as a liability.

How often should I update it?

Quarterly or annually is enough for a broad personal snapshot.
