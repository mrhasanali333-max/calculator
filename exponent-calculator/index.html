About this calculator

An exponent indicates repeated multiplication. Fractional exponents can represent roots.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the base and exponent.
  2. Select Calculate.
  3. Review the power.

Formula and Calculation Method

Example Calculation

2 raised to the third power is 8; a power of one half represents a square root.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the exponent be negative?

Yes. A negative exponent produces a reciprocal when the base is nonzero.
