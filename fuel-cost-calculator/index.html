About this calculator

Trip fuel cost depends on distance, vehicle efficiency, and the price of fuel. Use matching distance and fuel units, such as kilometres and litres per 100 kilometres or miles and gallons.

Important note

Traffic, weather, load, and driving style can change real-world consumption.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the trip distance.
  2. Enter the vehicle's efficiency and fuel price.
  3. Select Calculate and review required fuel and cost.

Formula and Calculation Method

Example Calculation

A 300 km trip at 15 km per litre requires 20 litres. At 1.50 per litre, the estimated fuel cost is 30.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use miles and gallons?

Yes, as long as distance, efficiency, and price use compatible units.

Why did my actual cost differ?

Efficiency ratings are estimates and may not reflect traffic or driving conditions.
