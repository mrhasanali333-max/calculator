About this calculator

This calculator compounds current savings and monthly contributions until the selected retirement age.

Financial disclaimer

Results are estimates, not financial advice. Returns are not guaranteed and taxes, fees, inflation, and market losses are not included.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current and planned retirement ages.
  2. Add current savings, monthly contributions, and an assumed return.
  3. Select Calculate and compare contributions with investment growth.

Formula and Calculation Method

Example Calculation

Starting with 10,000 and adding 300 each month for 20 years at a 5% annual return produces an estimate that separates deposits from growth.
