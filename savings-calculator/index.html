About this calculator

Regular deposits can grow through both new contributions and compound interest. This tool displays those parts separately.

Important note

Interest rates, account fees, taxes, and compounding schedules affect actual balances.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter an opening balance and monthly deposit.
  2. Enter the annual rate and saving period.
  3. Select Calculate to see the projected balance.

Formula and Calculation Method

Example Calculation

An initial 1,000 balance plus 100 monthly for five years at 4% produces a projected balance above the total deposited because of interest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this account for fees?

No. Add a conservative rate or separately account for fees when planning.

Can I change the deposit frequency?

This version models regular monthly contributions.
