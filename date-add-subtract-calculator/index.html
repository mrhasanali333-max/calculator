About date arithmetic

Positive amounts move forward and negative amounts move backward through the calendar.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Choose a starting date and unit.
  2. Enter a positive or negative amount.
  3. Select Calculate.

Formula and Method

Example

Adding 30 days to June 1 produces July 1 in a year where the calendar interval matches.

Yes, date arithmetic follows the browser calendar.
