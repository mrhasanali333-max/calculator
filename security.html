About Fast Calculator

Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability or suspicious security issue, please report it responsibly through our contact page:

https://fastcalculator.website/contact.html

Please include the following information where possible:

  • A clear description of the issue.
  • The affected page, calculator, or feature.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue.
  • Screenshots or other helpful technical details.

Responsible Disclosure

Please do not access, modify, download, or delete information belonging to other users. Do not perform destructive testing, spam, denial-of-service attacks, or activities that could interrupt the website.

Website Security

We work to improve website security through appropriate technical and administrative measures. Depending on the service and configuration, these measures may include:

  • HTTPS encryption for website traffic.
  • Secure hosting and website configuration.
  • Updates to website code and dependencies.
  • Appropriate protection of account information.
  • Review and correction of security issues when identified.

Third-Party Services

Fast Calculator may use third-party services such as hosting, authentication, analytics, or advertising providers. Their security and privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Response to Reports

We will review security reports and take appropriate action where possible. Response times may vary depending on the severity and complexity of the reported issue.

For security-related questions, please visit our Contact Us page.
