About unit price

Comparing like units helps reveal value when package sizes differ.

How to Use

  1. Enter each product's price and quantity.
  2. Select Calculate.
  3. Compare unit prices.

Formula

Example

A 6 pack for 12 costs 2 per item; a 10 pack for 18 costs 1.80 per item.

FAQ

Can quantities use different units?

Convert them to the same unit before comparing.
