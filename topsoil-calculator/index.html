Planning note

Soil settles and beds may have uneven depth, so consider a practical ordering allowance.

How to Use

  1. Enter length and width in feet.
  2. Enter depth in inches.
  3. Select Calculate to see cubic feet and yards.

Formula

Example

A 10 by 12 foot bed at 4 inches deep needs about 13.33 cubic feet.

FAQ

Why cubic yards?

Landscape materials are often sold by the cubic yard; 27 cubic feet equal one cubic yard.
