About this calculator

Payoff time depends on the balance, interest charged each month, and how much is paid. Extra payments can shorten the schedule.

Important note

Actual lender schedules may include fees, changing rates, or payment rules.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the current balance and annual interest rate.
  2. Enter the regular payment and optional extra payment.
  3. Select Calculate to estimate months and interest.

Formula and Calculation Method

Example Calculation

A balance of 5,000 at 8% with 250 monthly payments is reduced month by month until it reaches zero.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my payment is too low?

The calculator will indicate that the payment does not cover the first month's interest.

Does an extra payment help?

Yes. It increases the amount reducing principal each month.
