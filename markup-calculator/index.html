About markup

Markup is added to cost to set a selling price. Profit margin measures profit as a share of that selling price.

How to Use

  1. Enter cost and markup percentage.
  2. Select Calculate.
  3. Review selling price and margin.

Formula

Example

A 50 cost with 40% markup has a 20 markup and 70 selling price.

FAQ

Is markup the same as margin?

No. Markup uses cost as its base; margin uses selling price.
