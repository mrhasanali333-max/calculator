About this calculator

Quadratic equations can have two real roots, one repeated root, or a complex conjugate pair.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter coefficients a, b, and c.
  2. Select Calculate.
  3. Review the discriminant and roots.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a negative discriminant mean?

It means there are no real roots; the calculator shows complex roots.
