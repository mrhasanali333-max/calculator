GUIDE

How to Calculate a Percentage

Learn the basic percentage formula and how to find what percent one number is of another.

Percentage calculations are based on: part ÷ whole × 100. Example: 25 ÷ 200 × 100 = 12.5%. Use the dedicated calculator for quick results.

GUIDE

How BMI Is Calculated

Understand the BMI formula using height and weight.

BMI is calculated from weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. Enter your measurements in the BMI Calculator to get the result.

GUIDE

How Loan EMI Works

Understand monthly payments, interest and total repayment.

EMI depends on loan principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments. The EMI calculator provides the estimated monthly payment and total interest.

GUIDE

Simple vs Compound Interest

Understand the difference between simple and compound growth.

Simple interest is calculated directly on principal, while compound interest adds interest to the balance over time.

GUIDE

How Discounts Work

Calculate sale savings and final prices.

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage to find the savings, then subtract savings from the original price.

GUIDE

How to Calculate Profit Margin

Learn profit, margin and markup.

Profit is selling price minus cost. Profit margin is profit divided by selling price, expressed as a percentage.
