About this calculator

Return on investment compares profit with the total amount invested. Include fees or follow-up investments in additional costs.

Important note

ROI does not show the timing or risk of a return, so compare it with context.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the initial investment and final value.
  2. Add additional costs or contributions.
  3. Select Calculate to see profit and ROI percentage.

Formula and Calculation Method

Example Calculation

An investment of 1,000 that finishes at 1,200 with 50 in costs has 150 profit and a 14.29% ROI.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ROI include time?

No. Annualized return is a separate measure.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. A final value below the total invested produces a loss and negative ROI.
