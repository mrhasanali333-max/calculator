About this calculator

Standard deviation measures how spread out values are around their mean. Both population and sample forms are shown.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter numbers separated by commas.
  2. Select Calculate.
  3. Compare the population and sample results.

Example Calculation

Frequently Asked Questions

Which standard deviation should I use?

Use population standard deviation for the whole group and sample standard deviation for a sample.
