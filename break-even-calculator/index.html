About break-even

Break-even is the point at which contribution margin covers fixed costs and profit is zero.

How to Use

  1. Enter fixed costs and per-unit costs.
  2. Enter selling price.
  3. Select Calculate.

Formula

Example

With 1,000 fixed costs and a 10 contribution margin, break-even is 100 units.

FAQ

What if price is below variable cost?

No positive break-even point exists because each sale loses money before fixed costs.
