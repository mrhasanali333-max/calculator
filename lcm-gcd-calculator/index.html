About this calculator

GCD finds the largest factor shared by all entered integers. LCM finds the first positive multiple shared by them.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter two or more integers separated by commas.
  2. Select Calculate.
  3. Review the GCD and LCM.

Formula and Calculation Method

Example Calculation

For 12 and 18, the GCD is 6 and the LCM is 36.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I enter more than two numbers?

Yes. Separate each integer with a comma.
