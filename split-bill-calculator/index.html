About this calculator

Add a tip percentage, then divide the complete total evenly among the group.

How to Use

  1. Enter bill, people, and tip rate.
  2. Select Calculate.
  3. Review tip, total, and per-person share.

Formula

Example

A 100 bill with a 20% tip split between four people is 30 per person.

FAQ

Can people pay different amounts?

This tool assumes an even split.
